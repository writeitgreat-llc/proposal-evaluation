{# ── Cookie consent ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────── Asks one question, once, and takes no for an answer. Included only from base_public.html, i.e. only on the public funnel pages and only when nobody is signed in. What this banner is and is not asking for. These pages count visits anonymously for everyone, with no cookie and nothing stored on the device (see _analytics.html). That measurement needs no permission and does not stop if you decline. The ONLY thing on the table here is a single cookie that lets us recognise a returning visitor. The copy says exactly that, because a banner that implies "decline and we stop watching" when the baseline measurement continues regardless would be a lie, and an obvious one to anyone who opens devtools. Compliance rules this markup deliberately satisfies: * Decline is exactly as easy as Accept — same size, same weight, same position, no colour trickery. A "Reject" hidden behind a second click is the specific dark pattern regulators have been fining people for. * Nothing is pre-ticked and nothing is set before an answer. The cookie is written by the server, in response to the click, and never before. * The choice is withdrawable at any time — any control carrying data-consent-open reopens this banner. * A Global Privacy Control signal is treated as an answer already given. Someone who has set GPC has said no at the browser level; asking again is precisely what that signal exists to prevent, so the banner never renders for them (see analytics_collect.gpc_opt_out). The "how we handle data" link is rendered only when PRIVACY_URL is set. This app has no privacy policy of its own: /confidentiality is about the ownership of submitted work and explicitly says it is not a contract, so it cannot carry a cookie notice. Publishing one is an open item owned by a human (CONTRACT_ANALYTICS.md §7); when it exists, set PRIVACY_URL and the link appears with no deploy. Linking at a page that does not answer the question would be worse than not linking. Styles are inline here rather than in static/css/style.css so the banner is self-contained and identical to the marketing site's. #}
